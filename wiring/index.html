// wiring

Every wire.

One 18650, one 5 V boost and a Radxa ZERO 3W with no header: every module is soldered straight to its pads, one wire per pad. Hover a pin or a wire to light up where it goes. The same plan is in the build guide and in two Obsidian canvases you can download below.

Power path

charging never needs the switch on

GPS takes 5 V from the Radxa's pin 4; PN532, CardKB and the DAC run from the Radxa's 3.3 V (pins 1 and 17) so the I²C bus never sees 5 V.

Seen from above as fitted: the board sits upside down, pin 1 at the microSD end (the case's left). Grey pins are unused.
